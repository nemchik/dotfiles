## Private context

- `~/.claude/work/` and `~/.claude/oss/` may hold gitignored notes and instructions for my work and open-source contexts. When a task touches one of those contexts, look for relevant files there and take them into account. They are absent on some machines; carry on without them.
- Each folder under `work/` and `oss/` may have a `README.md` that says what is there and when to read it. When that context is in play, read the index first and follow it.
- Never copy anything from `work/` or `oss/` into a repo, a commit, a PR or any other public place.
